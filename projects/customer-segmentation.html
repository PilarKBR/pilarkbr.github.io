Customer Segmentation Using RFM Analysis and K-means with Prediction

2024 Data Science Startup Campus - Final Project

Project Overview

This project represents my final capstone project at Startup Campus, focusing on developing an objective strategy to understand customers in depth and identify groups of customers who have similar needs, preferences, or buying behaviors using RFM Analysis. Customer segmentation is then performed using K-means clustering, and the predicted results of their total spending are forecasted using ARIMA time series analysis.

Objectives

  • Understand customer behavior patterns through comprehensive data analysis
  • Implement RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis for customer scoring
  • Apply K-means clustering algorithm for customer segmentation
  • Predict future spending patterns using ARIMA time series forecasting
  • Develop actionable business strategies for each customer segment

Methodology

1. RFM Analysis

RFM analysis evaluates customers based on three key metrics:

  • Recency: How recently a customer made a purchase
  • Frequency: How often a customer makes purchases
  • Monetary: How much money a customer spends

2. K-means Clustering

Applied K-means clustering algorithm on RFM scores to identify distinct customer segments. The optimal number of clusters was determined using the elbow method and silhouette analysis.

3. ARIMA Forecasting

Implemented ARIMA (AutoRegressive Integrated Moving Average) model to predict future spending patterns for each customer segment.

Key Findings

Cluster Analysis Results

The K-means algorithm identified 5 distinct customer clusters with the following characteristics:

Bottom Tier (Cluster 3)

Loyal Prospective Users with low total transactions and lowest spending compared to other clusters. These customers show potential but need engagement strategies.

Middle to Lower (Cluster 4)

Loyal Prospective Users with the lowest total transactions and spending. This segment requires careful nurturing and targeted promotions.

Middle Tier (Cluster 0)

Loyal Prospective Users with medium-level transactions and expenses. This represents the core customer base with steady engagement.

Upper Middle (Cluster 2)

Customers with fairly low shopping frequency, but when they make transactions, both the amount spent and total transactions are high. These are seasonal high-value customers.

Upper Tier (Cluster 1)

Premium customers with low shopping frequency but the highest spending and transaction values among all groups. These are the VIP customers.

Business Recommendations

Actionable Strategies for Each Cluster:

Bottom Tier Strategy

Offer affordable promos on diverse item categories. Focus on increasing engagement through value-driven offers and educational content about product benefits.

Middle to Lower Strategy

Provide personalized promos (discount vouchers, cashback) for specific categories tailored to their past purchasing history over the last year.

Middle Tier Strategy

Implement personalized item recommendations based on transaction history and provide attractive promos on frequently purchased categories.

Upper Middle Strategy

Focus on seasonal targeting with trend-based recommendations and promos for seasonal/trending categories for the next month.

Upper Tier Strategy

Apply premium bundle methods for multiple product categories and provide exclusive services & benefits beyond other customer groups.
